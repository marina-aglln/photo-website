ABOUT

Marina Agliullina is a fashion, portrait, and fine art photographer. As an artist, her favourite themes to explore are womanhood, female physicality, emotions, nature, and urban life. She works with digital, analog, and mobile photography.

Her work appeared in Forbes Russia and other local media. Currently she works internationally outside of Russia.
